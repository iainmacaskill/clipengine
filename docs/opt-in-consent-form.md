# Clip Permission — Opt-In Form

> **DRAFT — for legal review before first use.** This wording is the permission
> record the whole rights strategy rests on (plan §2); have a lawyer sanity-check
> it once before any streamer signs. Plain-language by design: a form that takes
> a minute to read gets signed; a legal wall does not.

---

## What this is

We run **[NETWORK NAME]** — short-form clip channels on TikTok and YouTube that
turn Twitch streams into credited highlight clips. This form records your
permission for us to clip your streams. It takes about a minute.

## What you're agreeing to

- We may create short clips (typically 60–90 seconds) from your **public Twitch
  streams and VODs** and publish them on our TikTok and YouTube channels.
- **Every clip credits you** in the format you choose below, with a link to your
  Twitch channel.
- **Our channels are monetised.** We may earn revenue from platform creator
  programmes on the clips we publish. We're telling you this up front because we
  think permission only counts if you know what you're permitting.

## What you keep (everything)

- **Your content stays yours.** This is non-exclusive permission, not a transfer
  of any rights. You can keep clipping your own content, let anyone else clip
  it, and post anywhere you like.
- **It costs you nothing and asks nothing of you.** No exclusivity, no
  obligations, no changes to how you stream.
- **You can revoke at any time**, no reason needed — email us or DM us. We stop
  making new clips from your content **immediately**, and remove
  already-published clips of you within **[7] days** of your request.
- **You can exclude anything.** Sponsor segments, specific games, personal
  topics — list them below and we won't clip them.

## What you get

- A steady short-form presence on TikTok/Shorts with zero effort — every clip
  is a credited advert for your Twitch channel.
- Takedown-on-request, honoured fast, no argument.
