# Streamer Outreach — Email / DM Templates

> **DRAFT.** Personalise the [bracketed] parts — the first line especially. A
> template that reads like a template gets ignored; one genuine sentence about
> their content is what earns the next twenty seconds. Send from a real,
> reply-able address. Keep the whole thing under 150 words: streamers skim.

---

## Initial outreach

**Subject options** (pick per streamer, A/B over time):
- clips of your streams — with your permission
- want free TikTok/Shorts clips of your streams?
- [their game] clips — asking first

**Body:**

> Hi [name],
>
> [One genuine, specific line — e.g. "The ward-into-five-people moment from
> Tuesday's stream had me crying."]
>
> I run [NETWORK NAME] — we turn Twitch streams into credited short-form clips
> for TikTok and YouTube Shorts. I'd like to include your streams, and I'm
> asking first rather than just doing it.
>
> The deal, in one breath: we clip your public streams, every clip credits you
> and links your Twitch, it costs you nothing and asks nothing of you, you can
> exclude anything (sponsor segments etc.), and you can revoke any time —
> new clips stop immediately and published ones come down within days. Being
> straight with you: our channels are monetised, which is why we ask properly
> instead of clipping quietly like most do.
>
> If you're in, this form takes about a minute: [FORM LINK]
> If not, no worries — a one-word "no" and you'll never hear from me again.
>
> [Your name]
> [NETWORK NAME] — [channel links so they can judge the quality]

---

## One follow-up (7–10 days later, then stop)

> Hi [name] — quick nudge on my note below in case it got buried. One-minute
> opt-in, credited clips, revoke any time: [FORM LINK]. If it's a no, just say
> so and I'll close the loop. Either way, [genuine sign-off — e.g. "good luck
> with the sub-athon"].

**Never send more than one follow-up.** A non-reply is a "no" for now; leave
the door open by not being annoying. Log the outcome either way.

---

## Replies playbook

- **"Yes" / form completed** → `roster add` with the form reference as
  evidence, thank them, send them the first published clip when it goes live
  (this is the moment they become an advocate — many will share it).
- **"Yes, but no [X]"** → record X in the form's exclusions and the roster's
  `--exclusions`; confirm back in one line.
- **"What's in it for me?"** → answer honestly: free promotion and credited
  reach; we keep the clip revenue; if that's not enough for them, that's a fair
  no (or a future rev-share conversation if they're a must-have — plan Model B).
- **"No"** → thank them, log it, never contact again, and never clip them —
  a documented "no" is the one thing worse to violate than silence.
- **"Remove my clips" (from anyone, any time)** → `roster revoke` immediately,
  takedowns inside the promised window, confirm when done.
